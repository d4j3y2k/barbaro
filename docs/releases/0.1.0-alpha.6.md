# Barbaro 0.1.0-alpha.6

Alpha.6 makes peer acknowledgment depend on verified delivery to the receiving
model. It also improves Claude publication timing, isolates unreadable feeds,
shows project-wide advisory conflicts, and adds `barbaro doctor`.

## Changes

- Use `barbaro read context` and `barbaro read turn show` to acknowledge complete
  peer responses. Hooks bind each read to its invocation, native success, and
  model-visible output. Failed, truncated, oversized, or unsupported reads leave
  unread turns available. Exact pages accumulate only for the same field hash.
- Version 3 cursors preserve older unread gaps and partial coverage. Context
  exposes its bounded history window; `barbaro turn list` remains the exhaustive
  index. Legacy `context`, `turn show`, and evidence commands remain observers.
- Claude can publish a completed first turn at its own Stop within the bounded
  closing wait. Parallel calls, queued input, pointers inside response groups,
  and harness-serialized calls retain their validated publication behavior.
- A failed, unsafe, malformed, or resource-limited feed does not hide healthy
  peers. Coverage reports the failure and `await` does not claim quiet from an
  incomplete scan.
- Project-wide path claims and watcher conflict events expose advisory overlaps
  across workstreams without mixing their conversations.
- `barbaro doctor` reads effective user/project settings, skills, hooks, ignore
  rules, build identity, store health, and observed publication/delivery. It
  diagnoses configuration without changing it or enrolling sessions.

## Upgrade

Install `barbaro@0.1.0-alpha.6` with Node.js 22 or newer, then follow
[INSTALL.md](../../INSTALL.md) to refresh the shipped skills and check hooks at
your chosen scope. Delivery reads require matching alpha.6 hooks and CLI.
For hooks pointing into a development checkout, coordinate an idle/drain rollout
before rebuilding its running `dist/`; see [dogfooding](../dogfood.md).

Run `barbaro --version` and `barbaro doctor --project-root /path/to/project`.
A fresh setup needs a real peer exchange before delivery can be verified.

## Verification and limits

The implementation passed independent builder/reviewer checks: typecheck,
1,090 tests, packed installation with normal and disabled lifecycle scripts,
and live Codex/Claude publication and delivery exercises. Public regression
fixtures preserve the relevant transport and response-group structures with
synthetic or sanitized content. Private review logs and session bodies are not
part of the source release or npm package.

Delivery support is pinned to the evidenced Codex 0.153.2/0.153.3 literal-exec
shapes and Claude Code 2.1.261 hook shapes. The complete delivery envelope,
including its newline, is limited to 8 KiB. Codex native delivery inspection
currently reads at most 128 MiB per trace and 8 MiB per record; canonical feed
and record limits remain 64 MiB and 8 MiB. Exceeding a delivery bound preserves
unread state and leaves the read observer-only. These bounds are not retention
or redaction guarantees.

Claims remain advisory, coordination remains per checkout, and provider updates
can require adapter changes. See [ALPHA.md](../../ALPHA.md) for the public-alpha
limitations. Incremental native-trace inspection, surfacing the observed Claude
producer version in doctor, and transient handling of unsettled groups at Stop
remain follow-up work.
